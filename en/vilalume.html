B2B · Hybrid work

Vilalume

Your company's office, with a secretary for every person. Vila puts those in the office and those at home on the same map. Lume reads each person's emails, calendar and Teams and says what matters today.

Visit vilalume.com.br → Free pilot

The problem

Hybrid work swapped the hallway for dozens of tabs. Information is scattered across email, meetings, chat and tasks, and nobody knows for sure who is available, who came to the office today or what the other person is working on. Everyone spends the morning triaging messages to find out what really needs an answer. And existing virtual office solutions are expensive for the reality of many Brazilian companies.

Two offerings, one platform

Vila · the place

A 2D virtual office seen from above, with meeting rooms, café, focus rooms and desks, and avatars with name and status. On a mixed day, it shows the physical floor on one side and the remote Vila on the other.

Lume · the daylight

One AI secretary per person. It reads emails, calendar and Teams and delivers a summary of what matters today, what can wait and where there is a calendar conflict.

Vila: a place for the team, wherever they are

  • Both offices on the same screen. The real floor on one side, the remote Vila on the other. You can see who came in today and who is at home without asking the group chat.
  • Check-in, not tracking. Each person says whether they are in the office or remote. No Wi-Fi, no GPS, no time online.
  • Rooms with the Teams you already use. Enter the room, enter the meeting. Click a colleague, call them on Teams.

Lume: a light for each person's day

  • Your day, summarized. Before the first coffee: what needs a reply today, the meetings and what can be left for later.
  • Priority from someone who knows the team. Lume knows who your leadership is from the org chart and puts those requests first.
  • It proposes, you decide. Lume only reads. Nothing is sent, moved or answered without you.
  • Focus protection. When a person is in focus mode, non-urgent notices wait.

In practice

Privacy: looking after the team, not watching the team

Read-only

Microsoft permissions are read-only. Lume sends nothing.

No email storage

The text of emails and chats is read on the spot and discarded. Only a short summary remains.

No ranking

No individual productivity metrics. Numbers are always the team's.

Deleted at the end

Pilot data is deleted within 7 days afterward. Only the aggregate report remains.

Free pilot for teams using Microsoft 365

Up to 20 people, for 1 to 2 weeks. At the end, a report on what changed in the team: emails triaged, hours in meetings and in focus, calendar conflicts and a conservative estimate of hours saved.

Under the hood

Vila uses a 2D map in the browser with real-time presence and proximity video. Lume combines reading through Microsoft Graph with Claude language models for triage and drafts. Both products share a common core (login, org chart, routine and presence), which makes each an independent offering and, together, an ecosystem.

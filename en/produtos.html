The products

Four products, four everyday problems.

In short

The common thread is the method: find a real friction, build end to end and publish. The four ideas are very different, but they went through the same path.

Pick a product to see the problem, how it works and where it stands today.

Outside work hours, I build products of my own. Each one was born from a common frustration that seemed poorly solved: people in the same place who never meet, playlists that take too much work, teams scattered across tools and bar owners who don't know what their own dish costs.

How I build

All four follow the same cycle, always end to end and with Claude: from idea to brand, from copy to code, and from code to a live product.

IdeaAn everyday problem that bothers me and seems poorly solved.
ValidationUnderstand who suffers from the problem and what already exists before building.
Brand and copyName, visual identity and the way to explain the product in a few lines.
DevelopmentThe product itself, with security and privacy designed in from the start.
LaunchSite live, product open to testing and the four evolving in parallel.

Each product has its own project in Claude, with context, decisions and brand recorded. That is what lets me evolve all four at the same time without losing the thread.

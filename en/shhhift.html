shhhift

Say what you want to hear and the playlist is ready on Spotify or YouTube Music. By text, by a list of songs, by a path between two artists or by migrating from one platform to another.

Visit shhhift.com.br →

The problem

Everyone knows the mood they want, but few have the patience to turn it into 20 songs chosen, searched and organized. Whoever receives a track list in a screenshot or a chat has to look them up one by one. Whoever switches platforms loses their playlists. And whoever wants to move from one style to another usually makes an abrupt jump that breaks the mood.

Four ways to use it

1

Request

Write it your way, as you would tell a friend: “upbeat songs for Sunday family barbecue”. shhhift understands style, era and moment and returns a playlist of 20 songs.

2

Song list

Paste the songs as they are, from a screenshot, a chat or an old list. It finds each one and builds the playlist in the order of your list.

3

shhhift (the transition)

Pick the artist at the start and the one at the end. It finds the artists that connect one to the other and makes the change gradually, with no noticeable jump. Shhh + shift: the change nobody notices.

4

Migrate

Paste the link of a Spotify or YouTube playlist. It finds the same songs and creates the playlist on the other platform.

Transition example

From João Gilberto to Radiohead, without feeling the jump:

João Gilberto · bossa nova→ Caetano Veloso · tropicália→ Os Mutantes · psychedelia→ Tame Impala · psychedelic rock→ Radiohead · art rock

How it works in practice

1

Ask

Write what you want to hear, paste a list or pick two artists. Then choose Spotify or YouTube Music.

2

Review

See the suggested songs and remove the ones you don't want before creating the playlist.

3

Listen

Open the link and save it to your library. It is also kept in the Playlists tab of the app.

Privacy: nothing is created in your account

  • No access to your Spotify or Google. Playlists live in shhhift's own account on each platform and you only receive the link.
  • It doesn't read what you listen to and doesn't touch your library.
  • Your name and email don't appear on the playlist. On Spotify it is public; on YouTube, unlisted and only opens with the link.
  • Account deletion at any time, inside the app.

Under the hood

The transitions between artists use a graph-based recommendation model that connects more than 100 thousand artists by affinity. That map is what lets it walk from one style to another through close neighbors, instead of jumping straight over.

Preço Feito

The price that pays the bills. Send your purchase invoices and the menu: Preço Feito shows the lowest price each item can have without a loss, with the math open. All from your phone.

Visit precofeito.com →

The problem

Many people who sell food set prices by looking at the neighbor or by gut feeling. Few know what the dish leaving the kitchen really costs: ingredient costs change with every invoice, the monthly bills (rent, electricity, water, gas, internet, staff) must be spread across what is sold, and spreadsheets are a chore and age fast. The result is selling below what you should without noticing.

How it works

1

Invoices

Send the purchase invoices (XML, PDF or a photo of the receipt). Preço Feito separates each ingredient and calculates the cost per kilo, liter or unit. Without an invoice, you can type the value by hand.

2

Menu

Send the menu or product list as a PDF or photo. The AI suggests which ingredients from your stock each item uses, and builds the recipe sheet for you to just review.

3

Costs

Enter the business bills. The AI splits the month's cost across what you sell, accounting for prep losses, packaging and fees.

4

Price

See the minimum price of each item, the one that pays the bills without a loss. Tap any number and see the whole calculation, from the cost of the bun to the rent.

Why it is different

  • No spreadsheet. You send what you already have (invoices and menu) and the product organizes the rest.
  • The AI does the boring part. It builds the sheet for each item and spreads the monthly bills. You just review.
  • Every number explained. No black box: the price recalculates on the spot when you change a cost.
  • Made for people with no time, designed for the phone, at the pace of a bar or restaurant's day to day.

Who it is for

Bars and restaurants

Dishes, portions and drinks with a recipe sheet: every ingredient on the invoice goes into the dish cost.

Snack bars, cafés and bakeries

Snacks, pastries and recipes that yield several units, with prep loss and packaging in the calculation.

Small food retailers

Grocers, neighborhood markets, delis and produce stores: direct cost from the invoice plus a share of the store's bills.

The brand

The name plays on “prato feito”, the PF: a settled, honest, everyday price, in the language of people who work in the sector. The symbol is a plate seen from above with cutlery and a check in the center, with the lime highlighter-marker accent. The voice is clear and direct, without jargon, as in “The X-Salada needs at least R$ 22.40 to pay for itself”.

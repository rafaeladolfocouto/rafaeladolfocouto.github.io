Clube Oficina Caseira

Know exactly what to produce, what it costs and what to sell it for. One tested recipe a week, with a technical sheet, a cost sheet and a suggested price.

Discover the Club →

The problem

People who make sweets and food at home usually have three doubts at once: what is worth making right now, what each batch really costs, and what to charge. Without those answers, the recipe may turn out great, but the price is a guess and the profit is in the dark.

What comes with each recipe

1

Tested recipe

A step-by-step recipe tested in the team's kitchen before it reaches you.

2

Technical sheet

Ingredients, quantities and yield organized so you can repeat the production the same way every time.

3

Cost sheet

The detailed cost of each ingredient and of the full batch, with no spreadsheet to build.

4

Suggested price

A reference sale price that you can adjust to your region's reality.

  • Packaging and storage. Tips so the product reaches the customer in good shape.
  • Editable spreadsheet. To swap ingredients and redo the math with your own prices.
  • Promo kit. Ready-made material to present the product.

How the Club picks what to publish

The content is not random. The Club follows search trends and commemorative dates to point out what is asking to be produced. When a strong trend appears, an extra edition is added, and the “Weekly Radar” sums up what is trending. The members area is updated every Thursday.

Who it is for

Those who want to start

They need product ideas that already come with cost and price worked out.

Those who already sell

They want to refresh the menu and check whether current prices still make sense.

Those who produce to order

They want to reach commemorative dates with a tested recipe and a defined price.

Why it exists

The Club is the content side of a bigger idea: helping people who sell food decide with data. It talks to Preço Feito, which works out cost from purchase invoices, and uses the same method as the other products, starting from a real problem and building end to end with Claude.

Current status

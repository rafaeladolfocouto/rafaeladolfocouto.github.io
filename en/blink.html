Blink

Check in at the bar, the party or the event and see who is in the same place and open to meeting people. Now, not after three weeks of chatting.

Visit appblink.com.br →

The problem

Dating apps show you people miles away that you may never meet. Between the match and the first date, weeks of chatting usually go by, and along the way come old photos, fake profiles and the fatigue of endless swiping. Meanwhile, the right person may be two meters away, in the same bar, with nobody making the first move.

The usual apps × Blink

The usual apps

  • People 30 km away, whom you'll never cross paths with
  • Weeks of chatting before arranging a meeting
  • Old photos and profiles that don't match reality
  • Endless swiping

With Blink

  • Only who is at the same spot, right now
  • It's a Blink, just go over and say hi
  • A selfie of the night, taken on site, on the spot
  • Few people, all real and nearby

How it works

1

Check in

Just arrived? Pick the venue from the list or scan the QR code at the counter and take the selfie of the night. It is valid only for today.

2

See who is here

The list shows who is in the same place and open to meeting people, with name, age, bio and what each one is looking for.

3

Send a Blink

Like someone? Send a Blink. If it goes both ways, it's a Blink: the chat opens and you are just a few steps from each other.

You choose the vibe

Each person says what they are looking for that night, and only shows up to people who want the same thing.

Crush

For flirting. That glance from across the bar can now turn into a conversation, without walking in blind.

Resenha

For making friends. Arrived alone, moved to a new city or want to widen your circle? Find people who want to hang out and chat.

Connection

For networking. At the event, the coworking space or happy hour, find who wants to swap contacts and opportunities.

Safety and privacy by design

  • Nobody sees where you are. People only know you are in the same place; the exact location never appears.
  • Verified presence. Check-in confirms the phone is really at the venue, which makes fake profiles harder.
  • The selfie of the night disappears. Once you leave the venue, the photo is deleted. It only stays if it became a match's photo.
  • Invisible whenever you want. One tap and you leave the list, without having to leave the venue.
  • Report and block in one tap, with moderation reviewing every case. Accounts with several reports are suspended.
  • Only 18 and over, with date of birth and a selfie taken on the spot, at the venue.
  • Your data is yours. No selling data, no use for advertising, and deleting your account takes seconds.

For bars, venues and events

The venue puts the Blink QR code on the counter, creates Blink nights and becomes the place where stories begin. Patrons get a reason to come back; operators get a livelier place.

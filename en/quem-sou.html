Why I did it

Data is only worth something when it changes a decision.

In every company I've worked for, the question was the same: how to turn data into money, into a better experience or into a safer decision. In fraud and credit, it was risk. In NPS and CRM, the customer. In retail, monetization. At Smiles, the platform and the AI that give all of it scale. To me, data is a strategic growth asset, and the work is always the same: connecting what we know to what we decide.

The portfolio products are that same question applied to everyday problems, with one extra ingredient: building everything end to end, from the idea and the brand to the live product. It is also how I stay close to product, code and generative AI, putting into practice what I study and bring to my team.

Let's talk

I'm always open to good conversations about data, AI, platforms, governance or leadership: exchanging ideas, discussing market trends or learning about the challenges of digital companies and financial institutions that treat data as a strategic growth asset.
